Privacy Policy for Little Sparks

Effective date: 3 October 2026

Little Sparks is a children's activity app designed to help parents and caregivers find age-appropriate activities for children.

Developer: Little Sparks Studio

Privacy contact: [littlesparks386@gmail.com]

1. Information we collect

Little Sparks is designed to work without requiring users to create an account or provide their name, address, phone number, or email address inside the app.

The app may store information locally on the device to provide its features, such as selected activity preferences, child age settings, saved activities, and sound-effect preferences. This information is stored on the user's device and is not currently sent to Little Sparks Studio.

2. Children's information

Little Sparks is intended to be used by parents, caregivers, and families. The app does not require children to create accounts or directly submit personal information.

Any child-related settings entered in the app, such as an age selection, are used only to help filter and display suitable activities and are stored locally on the device.

3. Information we do not currently collect

Little Sparks does not currently require or collect precise location, contacts, photos, camera data, microphone recordings, or personal account information for the core activity features.

4. How information is used

Locally stored settings are used only to operate and personalize the app, including remembering preferences, saved activities, and sound settings.

5. Sharing of information

Little Sparks Studio does not currently sell, rent, or share users' locally stored activity preferences or saved activities with third parties.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because the relevant preference and saved-activity information is stored locally on the device, users can remove it by using the app's available reset or deletion controls, where provided, or by removing the app from the device. Removing the app removes its locally stored app data, subject to the device's operating-system behavior.

7. Security

Little Sparks is designed to minimize the amount of information it handles. Information used by the core app features is stored locally on the device rather than being transmitted to our servers.

8. Third-party services

Little Sparks may use platform services required to distribute and operate the app, such as Google Play and the Apple App Store. Those platforms may process information according to their own privacy policies.

If future versions of Little Sparks introduce additional services such as subscriptions, analytics, advertising, or other third-party services that collect or process user information, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are introduced.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. The updated version will be made available at the same public privacy-policy location.
